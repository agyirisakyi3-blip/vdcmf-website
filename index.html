About Us

Vision de Melbee Care Foundation is a non-profit humanitarian organization based in Ghana, dedicated to restoring dignity and transforming lives in vulnerable and underserved communities.

The Foundation was established to provide structured and sustainable solutions to the challenges faced by children, youth, men, women, widows, and low-income families.

Through a holistic and impact-driven approach, the Foundation focuses on education, vocational empowerment, health outreach, advocacy, and community development. Our aim is not only to provide support but to create lasting change by equipping individuals with the skills, knowledge, and opportunities needed to become self-reliant.

We are committed to integrity, compassion, accountability, and excellence in all our operations, ensuring that every initiative contributes meaningfully to the growth and well-being of the communities we serve.

Our Programs

Education

Providing access to quality education for underprivileged children and youth through scholarships and learning resources.

Vocational Empowerment

Equipping individuals with practical skills and trade training for economic independence and self-reliance.

Health Outreach

Promoting health and wellness through medical assistance, health screenings, and wellness programs.

Advocacy

Championing the rights of vulnerable populations and raising awareness on critical social issues.

Upcoming Activities

Breaking the Silence: Men's Mental Health Awareness

Raising awareness about mental health challenges faced by men, encouraging open conversations, and providing resources for mental well-being support.

Teenage Pregnancy Prevention in Rural Communities

Educational outreach programs for students in rural communities, focusing on reproductive health, life skills, and creating pathways for a brighter future.

Substance Abuse Awareness and Prevention Among Youth

Community workshops and campaigns to educate youth about the dangers of substance abuse and empower them to make healthy choices.
